Summary

We are Ashish Thete and Aayush More, directing the 3D animated short film Narsanhar (Part I). We have poured our complete time, energy, and heart into creating this story. This document represents our comprehensive pre-production package—capturing all scriptwriting, storyboarding, character blueprints, prop sheets, environment concepts, and production notes locked before entering active 3D production, animation, and final rendering.

Narsanhar Part I is a 4-minute, 54-second cinematic short film. It is set in a near-future world devastated by recurring armed conflicts. The civilian populace places its faith in a revered industrial tycoon, Aditya Gokhale, whose enterprise manufactures high-tech combat androids marketed as autonomous, clean, and ethical machine soldiers.

However, behind the patriotic marketing lies a grotesque corporate conspiracy: Aditya intentionally orchestrates warfare to maintain demand for his military contracts. Worse still, his "autonomous AI" robots do not run on artificial algorithms at all. They are powered by the extracted, living brains of vulnerable, impoverished citizens lured into false medical research trials under the promise of debt relief and food security. The narrative follows Unit-07, an active military android who begins recovering fragments of his lost human identity during a catastrophic trench battle, uncovering the forgotten life of a desperate worker named Agastya Prabhakar.

02

The Core Idea & Base Story

Original Core Idea • Part One

War for Profit & Stolen Human Consciousness

World is at war. Our hero character is a combat robot—Unit-07—having the consciousness of a human which he doesn't know yet. He follows his orders and fights on the frontline until a fatal explosion tears through his unit. Due to the catastrophic blast damage, his system malfunctions, making him remember who he actually was: an impoverished young man named Agastya Prabhakar who was lured by an advertisement promising free food and financial relief.

The antagonist is Aditya Gokhale, a wealthy industrial tycoon hailed as a national hero and savior. In truth, Aditya orchestrates the wars himself to fuel endless military demand for his robotic armies.

The darker truth is that his celebrated "autonomous AI" machines are completely fraudulent. He exploits desperate, starving people with no families or resources, harvesting their living brains to power his combat machines.

The story of Part One explores the awakening of this stolen humanity: the fatal frontline blast, the tragic domestic memory of Agastya's sacrifice, the clinical surgical horror of his transformation, and the dormant robot waiting in the quiet forest years later until a child's kick reboots his soul.

Three-Part Synopsis (Screenplay Flow)

Act I • Scenes 1 - 4 Beginning

The Trench War & Shockwave

In a blasted, muddy frontline crater, Unit-07 advances under shellfire alongside companion androids. Miles away in luxury, Aditya Gokhale directs the war over the phone while watching live television coverage. An enemy soldier fires an RPG, obliterating Unit-10. The violent shockwave hurls Unit-07 into the mud, causing a critical malfunction. Warm human memory flashes pierce his optical HUD. Damaged and leaking, Unit-07 crawls into the quiet tree line and shuts down against a boulder while attempting to upload camera telemetry.

Focus: Kinetic warfare, RPG impact, memory anomaly, forest retreat.
Act II • Scenes 5 - 15 Middle

Agastya's Despair & Conversion

We jump back into the memory of the human soul inside: Agastya Prabhakar, trapped in poverty and grief following his parents' death, slaving away at an endless corporate desk. Passing a bus stop at night, he spots an advertisement for Aditya Gokhale's medical trial promising relief. After a hungry, sleepless night, Agastya rushes to the lab and signs the consent forms. In the testing hall, participants are given "NIRA". Agastya hesitates, but sips the pungent compound—his vision warps into a terrifying 2.5D delirium before collapsing. A brutal surgical montage extracts his brain and bolts it into Unit-07.

Focus: Corporate isolation, domestic starvation, 2.5D distortion, cybernetic surgery.
Act III • Scene 16 Ending

The Forest Awakening

A hard match-cut transitions from the operating chamber to the tranquil forest years later. Unit-07 sits slumped against the rock, heavily rusted, covered in dirt and thick ivy. A young boy playing with a worn football accidentally kicks the ball against the robot's head. The force reconnects a dormant relay inside. As a mother's voice calls her son in the distance, Unit-07's internal systems receive power, and his optical sensor blinks and flares crimson red into consciousness as the screen cuts to black.

Focus: Peaceful epilogue, kinetic impact trigger, crimson sensor reboot.
05

Thematic Foundation

1. The Disposable Common Man

The film highlights how ordinary working-class individuals bear the brunt of geopolitical conflicts manufactured by the wealthy. Agastya is drained twice: first through mind-numbing office labor under flickering fluorescent bulbs, and finally stripped of his bodily autonomy to become a literal weapon in another man's war.

2. Flesh, Steel & Organic Memory

Can a human mind be permanently formatted like computer hardware? Narsanhar argues that love, grief, and memories of our parents are not merely chemical data—they are deeply ingrained human truths that survive even when the body is replaced by titanium plating and servo motors.

3. Visual Contrast: Grime, Sterility & Nature

Our visual language moves across three stark color palettes: the muddy browns, greys, and fire-orange of the warfront; the suffocating, cold blue-white of Aditya's corporate lab; and the lush, forgiving green of the quiet forest where nature reclaims the rusted machine of death.

4. The False Savior & The Media Machine

Aditya Gokhale represents the polished modern autocrat who weaponizes media broadcasts to appear philanthropic. While crowds cheer his speeches on television displays, the reality of his wealth is built on the silent slaughter of the very citizens who revere him.

The Full Story (Part One Flow)

Locked Screenplay Narrative • Part One

NARSANHAR • Complete Episode 01 Story

In a futuristic world torn apart by endless warfare, combat androids are deployed on battlefields under the command of industrialist Aditya Gokhale. To the civilian populace, Aditya is revered as a patriotic visionary whose technological inventions safeguard the country. Behind this polished public facade, however, lies a monstrous reality: Aditya intentionally stokes armed conflicts to ensure a continuous market for his military weapons, hiding his crimes behind philanthropic charity drives.

The darkest secret of all is that his celebrated "autonomous AI" war robots are not autonomous machines at all. They are powered by the living, extracted brains of desperate citizens.

The story begins on a pulverized, smoke-choked battlefield. Unit-07, an active military android, advances through deep craters alongside fellow unit Unit-10. Miles away in an executive penthouse, Aditya Gokhale sits comfortably in a leather armchair, casually tapping his fingers while watching live news coverage of the war on television and barking orders over a landline phone.

On the frontline, an enemy insurgent shoulders a rocket launcher and fires directly at Unit-10. A massive explosion erupts, and the violent shockwave tosses Unit-07 into the mud, heavily damaging his chassis. As internal diagnostics fail, warm flashes of human memories—a quiet room, late parents, and smiling faces—flood his optical display. Regaining consciousness, Unit-07 drags his broken frame through the mud and smoke into a nearby forest, slumping against a large boulder. He attempts an emergency telemetry upload to the server, but his power drops to zero and his system goes dark.

The Memory of Agastya Prabhakar

The narrative jump-cuts into the lost past: Agastya Prabhakar sits trapped inside an endless corporate office floor in Pune, surrounded by the monotonous clatter of keyboards and hundreds of silent cubicles. Crushed by debt, loneliness, and the death of his parents, Agastya stares blankly at his screen with throbbing headaches.

Walking home through the evening fog, he passes an electronics showroom window where television screens broadcast Aditya Gokhale delivering an inspiring national address. At a nearby bus stop, Agastya's eyes catch a recruitment poster: Gokhale Bio-Tech offering substantial financial compensation and food support for volunteers in a medical trial.

Inside his modest room, his stomach growls from hunger. He turns on the radio and looks up at the framed photograph of his late parents draped with a withered garland. Unable to sleep, tossing and turning through the night, Agastya resolves to take the risk. At dawn, he packs his few belongings, retrieves the address from the bus stop, and heads to Aditya's research facility.

At the information desk, a receptionist greets Agastya with a practiced smile. Overwhelmed and intimidated by the legal forms, Agastya hesitates, but signs the consent papers. Escorted through sterile white corridors and past secured doors, he enters the bright central testing chamber where several other impoverished volunteers wait on examination beds.

Aditya Gokhale personally welcomes the group, assuring them the trial is safe and rewarding. Lab attendants distribute glasses containing a chemical drink called "NIRA". When a worker accidentally bumps Agastya's glass, half of the liquid spills onto the floor. Agastya hesitates, smelling a strong, pungent odor. Around him, the other volunteers drink and immediately collapse limp onto their beds.

Agastya raises his hand in panic, but the small sip he swallowed takes effect instantly. His vision blurs, the laboratory walls bend and stretch, and his consciousness dissolves into a surreal 2.5D visual delirium as he collapses onto the bed.

A harrowing surgical montage follows: mechanized robotic clamps secure Agastya's body. Lasers open his skull, and Aditya watches with cold detachment as mechanical calipers extract Agastya's living brain, implanting it into an armored cybernetic neuro-processing chamber. Neural cables fuse with biological tissue, and the armored shell closes. Unit-07 stands assembled inside the pod—while beside it on the operating bed lies Agastya's lifeless human body.

The Epilogue in the Forest

Years pass. In the quiet overgrown forest, Unit-07 sits where he collapsed years ago, his chassis rusted, coated in moss, and wrapped in thick ivy. A worn football rolls across the grass and gently strikes the robot's leg.

A young boy runs over, picks up the ball, and curiously inspects the strange metal relic. Stepping back, the child kicks the ball with greater force, striking Unit-07 directly on the head.

The physical impact trips an internal power relay. A tiny indicator light blinks weakly. Deep within the oxidized armor, systems begin receiving power. As a mother's voice calls her son from down the trail, Unit-07's central optical sensor flickers once more, blazing into crimson red as his true human consciousness reboots.

CUT TO BLACK.

ACT-2 REALISATION

JUMP CUT TO:

INT. CORPORATE OFFICE - EVENING

Several sticky notes, reminders, and deadlines are pinned on a board above an office desk.

A tired hand repeatedly flicks a pen against the desk.

Depressed and worried AGASTYA sits on his regular office chair.

Beyond him stretches a massive office floor filled with long rows of identical desks.

Hundreds of employees work silently at their computers, creating the feeling of an endless corporate machine.

Computer keyboards click continuously. Phone calls echo across the office. No one speaks.

Agastya stares blankly at his monitor. His hand rests on his forehead as he thinks about his miserable life.

EXT. FRONT OF TV SHOP - EVENING

Street lamp flickers between evening fog.

All the TVs of the TV shop are playing different news channels showing Aditya Gokhale's speech in front of thousands of people.

Agastya, lost in his thoughts, passes by the TV shop.

EXT. NEAR BUS STOP - CONTINUOUS

Agastya walks home along the footpath.

As he passes a bus stop, his eyes catch a poster pasted nearby.

He slows down. For a second, he looks at it. Then he ignores it and continues walking.

INT. AGASTYA'S HOUSE - CONTINUOUS

Agastya opens the door, enters the room, and closes it behind him.

His stomach growls from hunger.

He keeps his bag on the chair. Turns on the radio.

A framed photograph of his late parents hangs on the wall, decorated with a flower garland.

He goes to sleep carrying the regrets of his life.

TIME-LAPSE:

He repeatedly changes sleeping positions, unable to sleep properly.

His stomach continues growling. He begins thinking about the poster he saw at the bus stop.

INT. AGASTYA'S HOUSE - MORNING

As soon as morning arrives, he packs his belongings.

EXT. NEAR BUS STOP - CONTINUOUS

He returns to the bus stop and searches for the poster.

After finding it, he immediately rushes toward the address written on it.

CUT TO:

EXT. ADITYA'S LAB - CONTINUOUS

The laboratory appears to be under development, but everything looks professional and trustworthy.

Agastya looks up at the structure with nervous hope.

INT. ADITYA'S LAB - INFORMATION DESK - CONTINUOUS

Agastya approaches the information desk. A RECEPTIONIST looks at him with a practiced smile.

He shows the poster to the receptionist.

She explains the procedure, reassures him, and asks him to sign the document.

Agastya looks at the papers. He does not fully understand them.

He looks back at her, nervous. She points to the signature area.

After a moment of hesitation, Agastya signs.

INT. ADITYA'S LAB - WAITING ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Agastya enters the waiting area and takes a seat.

A few other participants are already waiting quietly, each holding the same poster or registration documents.

After some time, the receptionist enters the waiting area. She takes him toward the lab.

He follows the receptionist through a secured corridor toward the experiment room.

INT. ADITYA'S LAB - CORRIDOR - CONTINUOUS

RECEPTIONIST leads Agastya through the corridor past heavy secured laboratory doors.

INT. ADITYA'S LAB - EXPERIMENT AREA - CONTINUOUS

The experiment area is bright and filled with advanced scientific equipment and researchers working at different stations.

Several contestants are already present.

At the center of the room stands ADITYA GOKHALE, welcoming the new participants.

He explains the purpose of the experiment and assures everyone that the procedure is completely safe. He then begins describing the rewards each participant will receive after successfully completing the experiment.

As part of the experiment, everyone is given a drink called "NIRA".

One by one, each participant receives a full glass. The participants are unaware of what it contains.

When a lab worker reaches Agastya, he accidentally bumps the glass while handing it over. Some of the NIRA spills onto the floor, leaving the glass only half full.

The worker quickly apologizes and asks Agastya to continue, assuring him that it is not a problem.

Agastya accepts the glass but hesitates.

Everyone drinks the "NIRA". But Agastya feels something wrong.

He smells the drink before taking a small sip. A strong pungent odor catches his attention.

One by one, the participants collapse onto the beds. Their bodies go limp.

Agastya, shocked, raises his hand, trying to react. His vision begins to blur.

The sounds around him become muffled. His heartbeat grows louder. A sharp pain shoots through his head.

The laboratory begins to stretch and distort around him. Walls behind him start to bend. Lights smear across his vision.

The entire world slowly transforms into a surreal 2.5D visual style, representing Agastya's fading consciousness.

His strength leaves him instantly. He falls onto the bed.

MONTAGE - CONTINUOUS

A rapid montage begins.

Industrial robotic arms secure Agastya's body to the operating table. Bright surgical lights illuminate the room.

Mechanical instruments descend from above.

His body is surgically opened from the center, exposing internal mechanical and biological structures.

Aditya carefully removes portions of Agastya's brain and places them inside an advanced neuro-processing system.

Neural cables connect the biological brain to artificial processors. Microscopic robotic devices rewrite his neural pathways.

Cybernetic components are implanted throughout his body. Mechanical limbs replace damaged human tissue.

A transparent containment chamber slowly closes around the newly assembled machine.

The containment chamber opens. Unit-07 stands inside the chamber.

The camera slowly pans beside the chamber. Agastya's lifeless human body lies motionless on the operating bed.

MATCH CUT TO:

EXT. FOREST - DAY

Unit-07 remains leaning against the rock.

A football rolls across the grass and gently strikes the rusted body of Unit-07, leaning against a large rock.

A young boy runs into the frame, picks up the ball, and looks curiously at the strange object covered in rust, dirt, and thick ivy.

Thinking it is just an old machine, he steps back and throws the ball again.

This time, the ball strikes Unit-07's head with greater force.

Due to force, a tiny indicator light flickers.

The robot's internal systems begin receiving power. The indicator light blinks weakly.

While Unit-07 slowly begins rebooting, voices are heard from off-screen.

MOTHER (O.S.)

SON, come here... Why you always do like this...

SON (O.S.)

Mama, look what I found!

Unit-07's optical sensor flickers once more.

CUT TO BLACK.

Set Design & Environment Art

Environment 01 • Exterior Trench

The Shattered Battlefield

Deep crater-scarred mud flats with tangled barbed wire, burning vehicle hulls, and thick black plumes of smoke. The lighting is an overcast slate grey pierced by muzzle flashes and explosive orange bursts. Ground materials feature thick displacement mud and wet puddles that react dynamically to robot footsteps.

Palette: Charcoal, Wet Soil Brown, Fire Orange, Steel Grey
Environment 02 • Interior Office

The Endless Cubicle Floor

A soul-crushing corporate labyrinth stretching into infinite perspective. Harsh, flickering 4000K fluorescent lighting illuminates grey cubicle dividers, endless stacks of paper documents, sticky notes, and humming CRT/LCD monitors. The composition emphasizes Agastya's insignificance within a dehumanizing capitalist engine.

Palette: Sickly Fluorescent Green-White, Dull Grey, Paper Beige
Environment 03 • Interior Facility

Gokhale Bio-Tech Secured Facility

Pristine, antiseptic white halls with frosted security panels and glowing LED track lights in the floor. The surgical chamber houses articulated industrial robotic arms, suspended stainless-steel surgical pods, and overhead ultraviolet sterilization lamps. The clinical perfection deliberately masks the horrific biological slaughter within.

Palette: Clinical White, Chrome, Cyan Glow, Warning Red
Environment 04 • Exterior Epilogue

The Reclaimed Forest Sanctuary

Years after the war, an ancient deciduous forest where nature has healed the scars of human destruction. Dappled morning sunlight filters through thick tree leaves. Unit-07 sits slumped against a massive granite rock, his metal body embraced by dense moss and English ivy vines. An atmosphere of peaceful stillness interrupted only by a child's laughter.

Palette: Moss Green, Bark Brown, Golden Dappled Sunlight, Rust Orange

Note on the Journey (Director's Statement)

"Making Narsanhar: Two Directors, One Vision"

A Personal Statement by Ashish Thete & Aayush More

When we sat down to conceive Narsanhar, we knew we did not want to make a safe, generic project. We wanted to tell a story that possessed genuine emotional weight—a story that tackled dark, uncomfortable truths about how our modern society treats human beings as disposable cogs in corporate and geopolitical machines.

Developing Narsanhar has been the most challenging and deeply rewarding endeavor of our creative lives. We worked not as hierarchy, but as equal partners, writing and rewriting the script across sleepless nights, debating every beat of Agastya’s quiet tragedy, and drawing out 147 individual storyboard panels to ensure that every single camera cut served a purpose. We wanted the kinetic horror of the opening trench war to hit with raw visceral punch, only to contrast it against the suffocating silence of an Indian office cubicle and the quiet, sacred beauty of the overgrown forest in the final scene.

The 2.5D sequence in Scene 15 was an intentional artistic risk. We wanted the audience to feel Agastya’s terror as his reality is surgically dismantled, bridging 3D modeling and surreal motion graphic distortion into a singular psychological nightmare.

With our pre-production pipeline now locked, assets constructed, and storyboards rigorously timed to our 4:54 cut, we are stepping into full 3D animation, character performance, lighting, and final rendering. We are pouring our entire soul into this film, and we hope Narsanhar stays in the viewer’s mind long after the screen cuts to black.
