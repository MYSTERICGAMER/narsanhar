## 📽️ Project Overview

**Logline:**  
*When a catastrophic battlefield explosion damages combat android Unit-07, it triggers memories of an impoverished young man tricked into a sinister medical trial—revealing that the nation's celebrated war machines are powered by living human brains.*

**Synopsis:**  
In a futuristic world torn apart by endless warfare, combat androids are deployed on battlefields under the command of industrialist Aditya Gokhale. Revered publicly as a patriotic visionary, Aditya secretly stokes conflicts to maintain demand for his military machines. Worse still, his androids are powered by the extracted, living brains of vulnerable citizens lured into false clinical trials. The narrative follows **Unit-07**, an active android who recovers fragments of his lost human identity as Agastya Prabhakar during a brutal frontline battle.
